In 97 years of the Academy Awards, how many nominations do you think women have received for Best Director?

The real number: just 0.

In 97 years of the Academy Awards, women have received just 10 nominations for Best Director. Barely 2.3% of the total.
That's only 9 different women, though. Jane Campion is the one nominated twice, in 1994 and 2022.

Best Director, 1928–2025 3.1% of all winners
In nearly a century of the Oscars, only three women have walked up to the stage to accept the Best Director award. Just 3.1% of all winners.
It took 49 years for the category to nominate a woman at all: Lina Wertmüller, in 1977. And 82 years for one to win: Kathryn Bigelow, in 2010.

Methodology

Data covers all Best Director nominees and winners from the 1st Academy Awards (1928) through the 97th (2025). Gender classification is based on public records and verified reporting. The data were enriched with Wikipedia photos of the directors. View on GitHub ↗
